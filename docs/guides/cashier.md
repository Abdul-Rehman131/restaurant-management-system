# Cashier guide (one page)

For counter staff, on the counter PC or a tablet. Words in **bold** are the exact buttons. Keys
in `code` work on a keyboard; press `?` to see them all.

## Start of shift

1. Sign in. The first time, choose your own password and tap **Save the new password**.
2. Tap **Start shift** (turns on the bill-request tone). Check that the receipt printer is on.

## Open bills

**Open bills** lists every unpaid order. A bill request comes with a tone and a "new bill
request" badge. Search with `/` (table, name or order number), move with the arrow keys, and
open a bill with `Enter`.

## Take payment

1. Open the bill and check the lines and the **Total**.
2. Tap **Pay** (`P`), then choose:
   - **Cash** (`1`): type **Cash received** (or tap **Exact**). The app shows **Change due**.
   - **Card** (`2`): the card reference is optional.
   - **Split** (`S`): a **Cash part** and a **Card part** that add up to the amount due.
3. Tap **Pay and print**. The receipt prints, the order completes and the table is free again.
   To print again: **Preview receipt**, then **Print** (`Ctrl+P`).

## Counter order (Take Away)

**New counter order** (`N`): choose **Take Away**, type the **Customer name** (the phone is
optional), add items, then **Send to Kitchen**. When it shows **Ready for pickup**, take the
payment and tap **Hand over**.

## Other bill actions

- **Cancel order**, or cancel one line: a reason is required. The kitchen sees the change.
- **Move or hand over**: another free table, another waiter, or both.
- **Mark served** and **Request bill**, when the waiter can't.
- Discounts and voided payments need an admin.

## Five common mistakes and fixes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Wrong payment method recorded. | Ask an admin to **Void** the payment (with a reason), then take it again. |
| "Less than the amount due", or the split won't save. | Check the cash typed, or make the two parts add up exactly to the amount due. |
| The receipt did not print. | **Preview receipt**, then **Print**. Check the paper and the printer power. |
| A guest asks for a discount. | Only an admin can give one. Ask the admin; the bill updates by itself. |
| "This bill is closed", or an offline bar. | It was paid or cancelled on another screen: go back to **Open bills**. If offline, wait for the bar to clear. Never take the money twice. |
