# Kitchen guide (one page)

For chefs, on the kitchen tablet. Words in **bold** are the exact buttons.

## Start of shift

1. Sign in with your username and password. The first time, choose your own password (at
   least 10 characters) and tap **Save the new password**.
2. Tap **Start shift**. This turns on the new-ticket chime; the button next to it shows
   **Sound on** or **Muted**.

## The tickets

- Each card is one ticket: the table (T5) or **Take Away**, the items, the notes, and how many
  minutes ago it was sent. No prices are shown.
- **ADD-ON** means extra food for a table that already ordered: cook only what is on that card.
- Colours: **On time**, then **Waiting** (amber), then **Late** (red). The admin sets the minutes.
- Bottled drinks and other items that need no cooking never appear here. That is correct.

## Cook and send

1. Tap **Start** when you begin a ticket. It shows **Preparing**.
2. Tap **Ready** when it is plated. The waiter gets an alert at once, and the ticket moves to
   **Recently ready**.

## Sold out

Tap **Sold out list**, search the item and switch it to **Sold out**. It disappears from every
ordering screen at once. Switch it back to **Available** when you have it again.

## Five common mistakes and fixes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| I tapped **Ready** too early. | Ready can't be undone. Tell the waiter to wait for your word. |
| A ticket I was cooking is gone. | It was marked Ready or cancelled. Look in **Recently ready**; if it is not there, ask the cashier. |
| No chime for new tickets. | Tap **Start shift**, check it says **Sound on**, and turn the tablet volume up. |
| An item ran out but waiters keep ordering it. | Mark it in **Sold out list**. Orders already sent still show it: tell the waiter. |
| "The kitchen queue could not be loaded", or an offline bar. | Check the Wi-Fi, then tap **Try again**. Use paper slips while it is down; the screen catches up by itself. |
