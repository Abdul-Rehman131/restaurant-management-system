# Waiter guide (one page)

For floor staff, on your phone. Words in **bold** are the exact buttons.

## Start of shift

1. Sign in with the username and password your manager gave you. The first time, the app asks
   you to choose your own password (at least 10 characters, not your username). Tap **Save the
   new password**.
2. Tap **Start shift**. This turns on the Ready sound and keeps the screen awake. Keep the app
   open during the shift.

## Take an order

1. **Tables**: tap a free table. "My tables" shows your tables and the free ones.
2. Tap a category or search, then tap an item to add it. Tap again for one more.
3. Tap **Review** to change quantities or add a note for the kitchen (for example: no onion).
4. Tap **Send to Kitchen**. "Sent to the kitchen" with the order number means it arrived.

## During the meal

- When the kitchen marks food Ready, you hear a sound and see **Table N is ready**. **Alerts**
  lists all of them.
- Serve the food, then open the table and tap **Mark served**.
- More food: open the table, tap **Add more items**, choose, then **Send add-on**.
- Guests change tables: **Move table**, then choose a free table. The bill moves with them.
- Guests want to pay: tap **Request bill**. The cashier sees it at once.

## Five common mistakes and fixes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| I added a wrong item and have **not sent** yet. | Tap **Review** and remove the line (or lower its quantity). Then send. |
| I sent a wrong item. | You can't remove it after sending. Ask the cashier to cancel the line (they need a reason). |
| An item says **Sold out** and I can't send. | Remove it in **Review** ("Remove the sold-out items to send"), tell the guest, then send. |
| "Table N is served by another waiter." | Ask the cashier to hand the table over to you. |
| An offline bar shows, or nothing updates. | Don't keep tapping. Move closer to the Wi-Fi; the app reloads itself when it is back. A double tap never makes two orders. |

Forgot your password? Ask an admin to reset it. You then choose a new one at sign-in.
