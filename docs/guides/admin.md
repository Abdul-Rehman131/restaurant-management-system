# Admin guide (one page)

For the owner or manager, on a laptop or phone. Words in **bold** are the exact buttons.

## Sign in

1. Sign in with your username and password, then enter the 6-digit code from your
   authenticator app. Admins always need this second step. The first time, scan the QR code
   with the app, then choose your own password.
2. Lost the phone with the codes? Another admin opens **Staff** and resets your two-step
   sign-in. Keep two admin accounts so this is always possible.
3. **Change password** is in the **Account** menu at the top of every screen.

## Every day

- **Dashboard**: sales, orders, average order and open bills for the business day, sales by
  hour and by order type, and the top 10 items. Sales count completed (served and paid) orders.
- Discounts and voids: open the cashier screen (the app address ending in `/cashier`), open the
  bill, then **Discount** (a fixed amount off, with a reason) or **Void** on a payment (with a
  reason). Both are recorded with your name. Admins can open every role's screen this way
  (`/waiter`, `/kitchen`, `/cashier`).

## Menu and tables

- **Menu**: **New item**, or edit an item: name, category, price, cost price, photo, **Goes to
  the kitchen** (off for bottled drinks) and **Available to order**. A price change applies to
  new orders only.
- **Categories**: **New category**, rename, and the order the staff see them in.
- **Tables**: **New table** with the number, seats and section.
- Nothing is deleted: **Archive** hides it, **Restore** brings it back.

## Staff

**Staff**, then **New staff member**: username, full name, role, then **Create**. Copy the
temporary password (it is shown once) and give it to the person; they choose their own at the
first sign-in. **Reset password** when someone forgets theirs. **Deactivate** someone on the day
they leave.

## Settings

Restaurant details, receipt footer, currency, **Tax (%)**, **Service charge (%)**, pricing
rules, rounding, **Day ends at** (04:00 by default) and the kitchen colours. After any change,
check a real bill in **Bill check**.

## Five common mistakes and fixes

| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| A price was wrong on orders already taken. | Old orders keep their price on purpose. Fix it for new orders; give a **Discount** on an open bill if needed. |
| A new staff member lost the temporary password. | **Reset password** gives a new one (shown once). |
| An item is gone for good but still shows. | **Archive** it. Sold out is for today; Archive is for good. |
| Bills look wrong after changing tax or service charge. | Open **Bill check**, type a real bill's lines and compare. Check the pricing-rule switches. |
| Someone left but can still sign in. | **Deactivate** them now: they are signed out everywhere at once. |
